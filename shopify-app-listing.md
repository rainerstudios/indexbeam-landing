## App Description (Long)

IndexBeam uses the IndexNow protocol to instantly notify Bing, Yandex, and other search engines when you publish or update products on your Shopify store. No more waiting days or weeks for search engines to discover your new pages.

### Key Features

**Instant IndexNow Submission**
Submit product, collection, and page URLs to search engines with one click. IndexBeam sends your URLs directly via the IndexNow protocol for rapid indexing.

**Auto-Index on Publish**
When you create or update a product, IndexBeam automatically submits the URL to search engines via webhooks. No manual work needed.

**AI Visibility Monitoring**
Track whether AI chatbots like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot mention your brand when users ask about your products or industry.

**Search Traffic Analytics**
See how much organic traffic your store gets from Bing, Yandex, and Google (via GA4 integration). Monitor your top-performing keywords and search positions.

**Index Health Dashboard**
Track which of your submitted URLs are actually indexed by search engines. See your indexing success rate and identify pages that need attention.

**Bulk URL Submission**
Submit up to 100 URLs at once for rapid indexing of your entire product catalog.

### How It Works

1. Install IndexBeam from the Shopify App Store
2. Your IndexNow key is generated automatically
3. Submit URLs manually or enable auto-indexing
4. Connect Bing Webmaster and Google Analytics for full insights
5. Monitor your search visibility and AI mentions
